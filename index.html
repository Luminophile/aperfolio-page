AperFolio

AperFolio reads the EXIF your camera wrote into your photographs and works out which body and lens made every frame — then shows you what you actually shoot with, where you made it, and what has been sitting in the bag.

Everything is computed on your device. Your photographs are never uploaded, and there is no account.
